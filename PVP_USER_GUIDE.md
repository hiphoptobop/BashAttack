# PvP Arena User Guide

Welcome to the **PvP Arena** — a fast, competitive mode where you bring your idle clicker hero into live battles against other players.

This guide explains how to enter the arena, how combat works, what your stats mean, and how to survive long enough to start stacking up eliminations.

---

## What Is PvP Arena Mode?

PvP Arena is the multiplayer combat side of the game.

Instead of fighting monsters, you fight **other players** using the character you built in the idle clicker mode. Your hero keeps important progression stats, so the upgrades you earn outside the arena still matter once the battle starts.

In PvP Arena, you will:

- Move around the battlefield in real time
- Click on nearby enemies to attack
- Watch your health and cooldowns carefully
- Respawn after being defeated
- Track your **kills** and **deaths**
- Use your upgraded character stats to gain an edge

> **Good to know:** PvP Arena is a continuous battle mode. There is no final match winner screen yet, so you can jump in, fight, respawn, and keep improving.

## Getting Started

### How to Enter PvP Arena

From the **Idle Clicker** screen:

1. Look near the **top-right area** of the screen.
2. Find the red button labeled **⚔️ Enter PvP Arena**.
3. Click it to begin the transition into PvP.
4. Wait for the loading message:
   - **“⚔️ Entering PvP Arena...”**
   - **“Preparing for battle”**

Once the arena loads, you will switch from the idle clicker interface to the PvP battlefield.

### What Carries Over From Idle Clicker?

Your PvP character uses the hero you already built in idle clicker mode. That means your arena performance is affected by your existing progression, including:

- **Health / Max Health**
- **Attack Power**
- **Level**
- **Tier**
- **Skill upgrades**
- Other persistent character progress tied to your player

This makes PvP feel like an extension of your idle clicker journey, not a separate character reset.

> **Tip:** If you want to feel stronger in PvP, spend some time improving your hero in idle clicker first.

## Controls

PvP Arena uses simple movement and click-based combat.

### Movement

Use either of these control sets:

- **W / A / S / D**
- **Arrow Keys**

### What the movement keys do

- **W / Up Arrow** — Move forward
- **S / Down Arrow** — Move backward
- **A / Left Arrow** — Turn left
- **D / Right Arrow** — Turn right

### Attacking

To attack:

1. Move close to another player
2. Hover your mouse near them
3. Click near that enemy player to attack

You cannot attack from anywhere on the map. You must be close enough for your strike to connect.

> **Important:** Clicking only works if an enemy player is close enough to be targeted by your cursor.

---

## Combat Mechanics

PvP combat is built around positioning, timing, and stat strength.

### Attack Range

Your attack has a limited range.

- You must be within **100 pixels** of the target to land a hit
- When you hover near an enemy, the game shows a visual range preview
- If the target is too far away, you will see an **Out of Range** warning

### Attack Cooldown

You cannot spam attacks endlessly.

- Each attack has a **1 second cooldown**
- After attacking, you must wait before attacking again
- Your UI will show whether your attack is:
  - **Ready**
  - or on **Cooldown**

### Damage

When you hit another player, damage is based on your character’s strength.

Your PvP damage is influenced by:

- **Attack Power**
- **Click Damage skill bonuses**
- **Level scaling**
- A small amount of random variation

That means two players with different idle clicker builds may feel very different in the arena.

### Health and Defeat

Every player has health.

- When your health reaches **0**, you die
- Dead players cannot attack
- Your character becomes inactive until respawn

### Death and Respawn

If you are defeated:

- A full-screen death overlay appears
- You will see:
  - **You Died!**
  - Who defeated you
  - A respawn countdown
- Respawn happens automatically after **45 seconds**

When you respawn:

- Your health returns to full
- You reappear at a safe location in the arena
- You can immediately rejoin the fight

### Kills and Deaths

PvP tracks your combat record.

- If you defeat another player, you gain a **kill**
- If you are defeated, you gain a **death**
- Your **K/D** is shown in the PvP UI

## Stats & Progression

One of the most important things to understand is that PvP is not disconnected from idle clicker progression.

### Stats That Matter in PvP

Your arena performance is affected by your hero’s existing stats, especially:

- **Max Health**  
  More health means you can survive longer in fights.

- **Attack Power**  
  Higher attack power increases your damage.

- **Level**  
  Your level contributes to PvP damage scaling.

- **Click Damage upgrades**  
  These upgrades also improve PvP attack damage.

### Skills and Upgrades

If you invested in your character in idle clicker mode, those upgrades can help in PvP.

Examples:

- A player with more **Vitality / Max Health** can survive longer
- A player with more **Strength / Attack Power** can hit harder
- A player with stronger **Click Damage** can burst enemies down faster

### What This Means in Practice

Here is a simple example:

- **Player A** enters PvP early with low health and low attack power
- **Player B** enters after leveling up and buying upgrades in idle clicker

Player B will usually have the advantage because their character stats transfer into the arena.

> **Tip:** If PvP feels difficult at first, return to idle clicker, improve your build, and come back stronger.

## UI Elements

PvP Arena gives you several visual tools to help you fight effectively.

### 1) Health Bars

Health bars appear above players.

They show:

- Current health
- Maximum health
- A color-based warning level:
  - **Green** = healthy
  - **Yellow** = getting low
  - **Red** = in danger

### 2) Damage Numbers

When attacks land, floating damage numbers appear.

These help you quickly tell:

- Whether your hit connected
- Roughly how hard you hit
- When multiple attacks are happening in a crowded fight

### 3) Attack Range Indicator

When you hover near an enemy:

- A circular range indicator appears around your character
- The indicator helps you judge whether the enemy is close enough
- You may see:
  - **Click to Attack!** when in range
  - **Out of Range** when too far away

### 4) Attack Cooldown Status

Your stats panel shows whether your attack is ready.

You will see:

- **Attack: Ready**
- or **Attack: Cooldown X.Xs**

This is one of the most important PvP indicators. If you click while still on cooldown, your attack will not go through.

### 5) Kill Feed

The kill feed appears on the screen and shows recent eliminations.

It tells you:

- Who defeated whom
- Which fights are happening around the arena
- Whether a dangerous player is on a streak

### 6) Death Overlay

When you die, the screen clearly tells you:

- That you were defeated
- Which player got the kill
- How many seconds remain until respawn

### 7) Stats Panel

Your on-screen panel includes useful combat information such as:

- Health
- Gold
- Level
- Tier
- Power-Up Points
- Attack Power
- Companions count
- **PvP K/D**
- Attack cooldown state

> **Note:** Some stats shown in the panel come from your broader character progression, even if they are more important in idle clicker than in PvP.

## How to Return to Idle Clicker Mode

At the moment, there is **no documented in-arena return button shown in the implemented PvP screen**.

If you want to leave PvP and go back to idle clicker mode, use the game’s normal navigation or session flow available in your current build.

> **Important:** Since this guide is based on the currently implemented behavior, do not expect a visible “Return to Idle Clicker” button inside the PvP arena unless your version adds one later.

If you are testing or switching modes manually, you may need to leave the current session and re-enter the idle clicker screen through the usual game flow.

## Tips & Strategy

Want to last longer and win more fights? Start here.

### Positioning Tips

- Stay close enough to attack, but do not drift carelessly into enemy range
- Use hover previews before clicking so you do not waste time on out-of-range attacks
- If an enemy is backing away, keep moving and wait for the right moment instead of panic-clicking

### Timing Tips

- Watch your **1-second cooldown**
- Attacking the instant your cooldown ends is often better than random clicking
- If your attack is not ready, focus on movement and positioning

### Survival Tips

- Keep an eye on your health bar at all times
- If your health is low, avoid extended trades unless you are sure you can finish the enemy first
- Red health means you are one bad exchange away from dying

### Build Tips

- Improve your idle clicker hero before expecting strong PvP results
- Prioritize upgrades that improve:
  - Health
  - Attack Power
  - Click Damage
- A stronger build gives you more room for mistakes in PvP

### Awareness Tips

- Check the kill feed to understand what is happening around you
- If one player keeps appearing in the kill feed, treat them as a serious threat
- Use respawn time to reset mentally and plan your next engagement

### Beginner-Friendly Strategy

If you are new to PvP:

1. Enter the arena
2. Practice movement first
3. Hover enemies to learn your range
4. Attack only when the indicator shows you are close enough
5. Watch your cooldown after every hit
6. Learn from each death and come back stronger

## FAQ

### What is PvP Arena?

PvP Arena is the multiplayer mode where you fight other players using your idle clicker character.

### How do I enter PvP Arena?

Click the **⚔️ Enter PvP Arena** button near the top-right of the idle clicker screen.

### Do my idle clicker stats matter in PvP?

Yes. Your character’s health, attack power, level, and certain skill upgrades carry into PvP.

### How do I move?

Use **WASD** or the **Arrow Keys**.

### How do I attack?

Move near another player, hover near them, and click close enough to target them.

### Why is my attack not working?

Common reasons include:

- The enemy is too far away
- Your attack is still on cooldown
- The target is already dead
- You clicked where no valid target could be selected

### How far can I attack?

Your attack range is **100 pixels**.

### How long is the attack cooldown?

The cooldown is **1 second** between attacks.

### What happens when I die?

You are defeated, a death overlay appears, and you automatically respawn after **45 seconds** with full health.

### Can I attack while dead?

No. Dead players cannot attack until they respawn.

### What does PvP K/D mean?

It means **kills / deaths** — how many players you have defeated compared to how many times you have been defeated.

### Is there a winner or end-of-match screen?

Not currently. The implemented PvP Arena is a continuous combat mode.

### Can I return to idle clicker from inside PvP?

There is no visible in-arena return button documented in the current implementation. Use your build’s normal navigation flow to leave PvP.

## Troubleshooting

### I clicked on an enemy, but nothing happened

Try this checklist:

- Move closer
- Hover first and look for **Click to Attack!**
- Check whether your attack says **Cooldown**
- Make sure the enemy is alive

### I keep dying very quickly

That usually means one of these is true:

- Your health is low compared to other players
- Your build is underpowered
- You are fighting while your cooldown timing is poor
- You are staying in range too long

Try returning to idle clicker, upgrading your hero, and then re-entering PvP.

### I feel weaker than other players

That is normal if they have progressed further in idle clicker mode. PvP strength is tied to character progression.

### The screen says “Out of Range”

Move closer before clicking to attack.

- **Character progression**
- **Positioning**
- **Timing**
- **Practice**

Do not worry if your first few fights are rough. Every upgrade, every respawn, and every battle helps you learn the flow of the arena.
