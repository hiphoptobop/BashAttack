Idle Clicker RPG - Game Guide

How to Play

Click the ATTACK button to damage monsters. Defeat monsters to progress through levels, earn gold, and gain power-up points!

Combat System

  • Attacking: Click the ATTACK button to deal damage to the monster
  • Monster Retaliation: Monsters attack back after you hit them
  • Health Management: Your health does NOT restore between battles - manage it carefully!
  • Death: If your health reaches 0, you respawn with full health at the same level

Progression

  • Levels: Defeat monsters to progress to higher levels
  • Power-up Points: Earn 1 power-up point every 5 levels (levels 5, 10, 15, 20, etc.)
  • Gold: Earn gold from defeating monsters (100% on victory, 5% on death)
  • Difficulty: Monster health and attack power increase with each level

Power-Ups

Use power-up points to unlock power-ups (max level 25 each):

  • Vitality (1 PuP): Increases your maximum health by 20 HP per level
  • Strength (1 PuP): Boosts your base attack power by 2 per level
  • Precision (2 PuP): Increases chance to do double damage by 5% per level
  • Leadership (2 PuP): Boosts companion damage by 20% per level
  • Fortune (2 PuP): Increases gold earned by 10% per level
  • Regeneration (2 PuP): Restores 1 HP per second per level

Companions

Purchase companions with gold to auto-attack monsters:

  • Warrior: Deals 2 damage every 1 second (Cost: 20 gold)
  • Archer: Deals 4 damage every 1 second (Cost: 100 gold)
  • Mage: Deals 16 damage every 2 seconds (Cost: 400 gold)
  • Priest: Deals 10 damage and heals you for 2 HP every 1 second (Cost: 1000 gold)

Note: Companion damage is affected by your Leadership power-up level!

Tips & Strategy

  • Balance offense (Strength, Precision) with defense (Vitality, Regeneration)
  • Invest in companions early for passive damage while you're away
  • The Priest companion is crucial for long-term survival
  • Don't forget - health doesn't restore between battles!
  • Leadership power-up makes all your companions more powerful
  • Fortune power-up helps you earn gold faster for more companions
